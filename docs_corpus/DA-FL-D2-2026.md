# Distributor agreement DA-FL-D2-2026: Sunshine Coast Convenience Network (FL-D2)

Parties: Acme Beverages (supplier) and Sunshine Coast Convenience Network (distributor ID FL-D2), Traditional Trade distributor for Florida.

Term: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31, renewable for one year.

## 1. Scope

FL-D2 distributes the full Acme Beverages range to convenience stores, bodegas and small independent grocers in the Sunshine Coast region. Beer (Harbor brand) is included under the distributor's Florida beer wholesaler licence, in line with the three-tier system (supplier → licensed distributor → licensed retailer).

Key SKUs: Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle (HB-LAGR-355B), Harbor Light 355ml Can (HB-LITE-355C), Fizzo Cola 2L PET (FZ-COLA-2000P), Aqua Pura Still 1.5L PET (AP-STILL-1500P).

## 2. Pricing and trade terms

List price per case as per the Acme Beverages FL price list. Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle: USD 21.60 per case of 24.

Off-invoice discount: 6% on all SKUs. Growth rebate: 2% on annual volume above the 2025 base.

Promo co-funding: 40% of the shelf discount for approved promotions, settled by credit note within 30 days of promo end.

## 3. Ordering and delivery

Regular orders: weekly. Minimum order: 50 cases per SKU, as in POL-REPL-2026.

Lead time: 6 days from order confirmation to the FL-D2 warehouse in Santiago.

Cover target: 14 days (Traditional Trade), as in POL-REPL-2026.

## 4. Beer freshness

Harbor beer is date-coded. FL-D2 must rotate stock first-in, first-out and pull any Harbor product older than 120 days from retail shelves, with at least 90% freshness compliance in store audits. Pulled stock older than 120 days is credited at USD 3.10 per case only when reported within 14 days.

## 5. Credit terms

Payment terms: 30 days from invoice.

Credit limit: USD 85,000.

Credit hold: Acme Beverages may place FL-D2 on credit hold if any invoice is overdue for more than 45 days, or if exposure goes above the credit limit. While on credit hold, no top-up or regular shipments are released, except cash-in-advance orders approved by the FL market finance controller.

## 6. Credit hold note (Finance, 2026-10-28)

Status: ON CREDIT HOLD since 2026-10-28.

Reason: three invoices overdue by more than 45 days (oldest from 2026-09-04) and total exposure of USD 97,400, above the USD 85,000 credit limit.

Effect: all replenishment requests for FL-D2 must be rejected with reason "distributor on credit hold", including top-ups of Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle (HB-LAGR-355B) even if the position is AT RISK.

Path to release: payment of the overdue invoices, or a payment plan signed by FL-D2 and approved by the FL market finance controller. Finance expects a decision by 2026-11-20.

Guidance to planners: cover convenience-store demand for Harbor Lager in the Sunshine Coast region through Sunshine Coast Retail Partners (FL-D1) and Sunshine Coast HoReCa Supply (FL-D3), within their own cover targets. Do not ship to FL-D1 for re-sale to FL-D2 customers.

## 7. Performance

Quarterly business review with the FL market sales lead. KPIs: depletions growth, numeric distribution in convenience stores, beer freshness compliance, days sales outstanding.

## 8. Termination

Either party may end the agreement with 90 days' written notice. Acme Beverages may end it with 30 days' notice if the distributor stays on credit hold for more than 90 days.
