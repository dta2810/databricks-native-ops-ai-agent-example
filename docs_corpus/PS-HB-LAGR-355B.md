# Product spec: Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle (HB-LAGR-355B)

## Identification

SKU: HB-LAGR-355B. Brand: Harbor. Category: Beer. Product name: Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle. Pack type: Glass.

Sister SKUs: Harbor Lager 473ml Can (HB-LAGR-473C), Harbor Light 355ml Can (HB-LITE-355C), Harbor Island IPA 355ml Bottle (HB-IPA-355B).

## Pack hierarchy

Unit: 355ml amber non-returnable glass bottle with crown cap.

Case: 24 bottles per corrugated case (4 × 6-pack carriers, case code HB-CS-24).

Pallet: 50 cases per pallet (5 layers × 10 cases).

List price per case: USD 21.60 (excluding state beer excise and any state bottle deposit).

Bottle deposit: charged only in states with container-deposit laws (NY in our footprint, USD 0.05 per bottle); none in FL.

## Product

Pale lager, 4.6% ABV. Brewed with barley malt and hops. Sold only through licensed distributors to licensed retailers (three-tier system). Legal drinking age and responsible drinking messages are required on pack and POS material in every market.

Shelf life: 9 months. Minimum remaining shelf life at delivery to distributor: 6 months. Store cool and dark; light exposure spoils flavour.

## Seasonality

Harbor demand peaks in December (about +25% versus the average month) with holiday gatherings, mostly in the Southeast markets (FL, NC) and On-Premise channels. Replenishment planning for December must start in early November.

## Channel guidance

On-Premise is the strongest channel for Harbor (about 2.2x the volume per outlet of other brands). In Florida, convenience stores (Traditional Trade) are also key for single-serve and take-home beer purchases.

## Freshness and rotation

Distributors must rotate stock first-in, first-out and pull beer older than 120 days. The freshness compliance target is 90% (see each distributor agreement, for example DA-FL-D2-2026). Freshness pulls cut sellable stock and can limit Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle availability in the next cycle.

## Out-of-stock impact

Harbor Lager shoppers at convenience stores often switch to the 473ml can (HB-LAGR-473C) if available, which limits lost sales. On-Premise outlets switch to competing beers.
