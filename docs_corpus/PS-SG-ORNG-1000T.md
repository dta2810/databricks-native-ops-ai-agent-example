## Pack details for SG-ORNG-1000T

Unit: 1-litre aseptic Tetra carton with screw cap.

Case: 12 cartons in a shelf-ready tray (tear-off front for direct shelf placement).

Pallet: 80 cases per pallet (8 layers × 10 cases).

## Product

Orange juice from concentrate, 100% juice, no added sugar. Vitamin C fortified.

Shelf life: 10 months ambient, unopened. Once opened, keep refrigerated and use within 5 days.

Minimum remaining shelf life at delivery to distributor: 6 months.

## Merchandising

The shelf-ready tray fits both the regular juice shelf and the Sunny Grove secondary floor display (POS kit SG-DSP-FLR-24, holds 24 cases). The Sunny Grove counter display (SG-DSP-CTR-06) holds 6 cases and is designed for convenience-store counters.

Display + feature promotions only deliver their expected uplift when the floor display is installed. On the regular shelf, the 1L Tetra competes with private-label juices and responds mainly to price.

## Channel guidance

Modern Trade: family shopping missions, strong response to displays. Traditional Trade: smaller baskets, responds to counter displays and free-case incentives. On-Premise: breakfast buffets and juice bars in hotels and cafés.

## Supply

Produced by a contract packer in California for the West and Southwest regions. Lead time to California distributors: 3 days. Minimum production run: 4,000 cases.
