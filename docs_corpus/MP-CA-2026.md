# Market playbook 2026: California (CA)

## Market at a glance

California is the largest Acme Beverages market (about 3x the volume of Texas). Region: West. Currency: USD.

Distributors: Golden Coast Retail Partners (CA-D1, Modern Trade), Golden Coast Convenience Network (CA-D2, Traditional Trade), Golden Coast HoReCa Supply (CA-D3, On-Premise).

Lead time from plant to distributors: 3 days, the shortest in the network.

## Brand priorities

Aqua Pura: protect leadership in the 500ml single-serve pack (AP-STILL-500P). Keep at or above cover target in Traditional Trade at all times.

Sunny Grove: grow the 1L Tetra (SG-ORNG-1000T) through Display + feature promotions in Modern Trade, and counter displays in convenience stores.

Fizzo: hold share in 600ml PET; front-of-pack sugar warning labels limit Modern Trade feature space.

Volt and Harbor: secondary in California; no promotions planned in Q4.

## Promotion rules for California

Every Display + feature promotion needs a confirmed merchandising plan from each distributor before launch, including the crew schedule for display installation.

The display installation deadline is the Friday of promo week 1 for Modern Trade and the Monday of week 2 for Traditional Trade and On-Premise.

In-flight review: if any distributor is more than 20 points below the uplift forecast after two full weeks, the market sales lead runs an in-flight review with the distributor and the revenue manager within 3 business days.

Lesson from 2025: Display + feature promotions that miss the display deliver about the same uplift as a plain price discount, at a higher cost. Fix execution before changing the price.

## Replenishment rules for California

Follow POL-REPL-2026. Cover targets: CA-D1 21 days, CA-D2 14 days, CA-D3 10 days.

During promotions, rebalance stock between distributors weekly based on measured uplift, not forecast uplift. Transfers within California take 1 to 2 days.

CA-D2 orders spike after free-case incentives. Watch CA-D2 cover on promoted SKUs and on Aqua Pura Still 500ml PET (AP-STILL-500P).

## Q4 2026 calendar

PR-0422 Sunny Grove Orange 1L Tetra, Display + feature, 20% off, 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-29 (see PB-CA-PR-0422).

Holiday season build: start December stock build for Sunny Grove and Aqua Pura in the week of 2026-11-23.

## Escalation roles

California market sales lead (commercial issues and promo execution). California supply planner (replenishment up to 500 cases). Supply planning manager (above 500 cases). West region revenue manager (promo investment).
