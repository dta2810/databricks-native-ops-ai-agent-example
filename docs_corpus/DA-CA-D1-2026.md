# Distributor agreement DA-CA-D1-2026: Golden Coast Retail Partners (CA-D1)

Parties: Acme Beverages (supplier) and Golden Coast Retail Partners (distributor ID CA-D1), Modern Trade distributor for California.

Term: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

## 1. Scope

CA-D1 serves supermarket and hypermarket chains in California, including 140 promo-eligible supermarket doors. It distributes the full Acme Beverages range.

## 2. Trade terms

Off-invoice discount: 7% on all SKUs. Growth rebate: 2.5% on volume above the 2025 base.

Promo co-funding: 60% Acme Beverages and 40% CA-D1 for approved promotions, settled by credit note within 30 days of promo end, subject to the execution compliance in clause 6.

## 3. Ordering and delivery

Regular orders: twice a week. Minimum order: 50 cases per SKU (POL-REPL-2026). Lead time: 3 days. Cover target: 21 days (Modern Trade).

## 4. Credit terms

Payment terms: 45 days. Credit limit: USD 260,000. Good standing as of 2026-11-01.

## 5. Merchandising

CA-D1 is responsible for merchandising crews in its doors, including installation of POS material supplied by Acme Beverages. Any change of merchandising contractor must be communicated to the California market sales lead at least 30 days in advance.

## 6. Promotion execution compliance

6.1 For each approved promotion, CA-D1 must meet the execution requirements in the promo brief (display, feature, price and stock).

6.2 CA-D1 must send a weekly compliance file listing every promo door and whether the display is installed, the feature ran and the price is correct. Doors that miss the display installation deadline must be flagged in the first weekly file after the deadline.

6.3 If display compliance is below the promo brief requirement two weeks after the deadline, CA-D1 must fund extra merchandising crews to close the gap within 10 days.

6.4 Promo co-funding from Acme Beverages is paid in proportion to compliant doors. Example: with 22% display compliance against an 85% requirement, Acme Beverages pays 26% of its planned display co-funding (22 / 85).

6.5 Repeated non-compliance (two promotions in a 12-month period) allows Acme Beverages to move future display promotions to another channel.

## 7. Excess stock

When CA-D1 holds more than 150% of its cover target for a SKU, Acme Beverages may hold further shipments of that SKU and request a transfer to other California distributors under section 9 of POL-REPL-2026.

## 8. Reviews

Quarterly business review with the California market sales lead and the West region revenue manager.
