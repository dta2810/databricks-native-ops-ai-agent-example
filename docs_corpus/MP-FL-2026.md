# Market playbook 2026: Florida (FL)

## Market at a glance

Florida is the largest Southeast market for Acme Beverages. Region: Southeast. Currency: USD.

Distributors: Sunshine Coast Retail Partners (FL-D1, Modern Trade), Sunshine Coast Convenience Network (FL-D2, Traditional Trade, convenience and independent stores), Sunshine Coast HoReCa Supply (FL-D3, On-Premise).

Lead time from plant to distributors: 6 days, by sea freight and road.

## Brand priorities

Harbor: Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle (HB-LAGR-355B) is the hero SKU in FL, sold mainly in 6-packs and 24-packs through convenience stores and On-Premise outlets.

Fizzo: Fizzo Cola 2L PET for family occasions.

Aqua Pura: 1.5L PET for households.

## Holiday season

December demand for Harbor is about 25% above the monthly average, driven by holiday gatherings. The December stock build for Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle must be shipped by 2026-11-30 because of the 6-day lead time and port congestion in December.

Freshness compliance must stay above 90% in November, so December stock reaches shelves fresh. Florida hurricane season ends on November 30; keep the post-storm safety stock until then.

## Replenishment rules for FL

Follow POL-REPL-2026. Cover targets: FL-D1 21 days, FL-D2 14 days, FL-D3 10 days.

Positions with cover below 6 days (the FL lead time) are URGENT: they will stock out before any new shipment arrives.

## Credit hold handling (FL-D2)

Sunshine Coast Convenience Network (FL-D2) is on credit hold since 2026-10-28 (see DA-FL-D2-2026, section 6). No replenishment is shipped to FL-D2 until Finance releases the hold, except cash-in-advance orders approved by the FL market finance controller.

When FL-D2 positions are AT RISK, the supply planner must reject the request with the reason "distributor on credit hold" and record the decision. Then the planner looks at covering convenience-store demand through FL-D1 and FL-D3, within their own cover targets.

Do not route stock through FL-D1 for re-sale to FL-D2 customers. This breaks the credit hold and the FL-D1 agreement.

The FL market sales lead informs key convenience-store owners about possible gaps on Harbor Lager and suggests Harbor Lager 473ml Can (HB-LAGR-473C) as an alternative where FL-D1 or FL-D3 can serve them.

## Q4 2026 priorities

Recover Harbor Lager 355ml Bottle cover at FL-D1 and FL-D3 before the December build.

Resolve the FL-D2 credit hold. Finance expects a decision by 2026-11-20.

## Escalation roles

FL market sales lead, FL supply planner, FL market finance controller (credit hold and cash-in-advance), supply planning manager.
