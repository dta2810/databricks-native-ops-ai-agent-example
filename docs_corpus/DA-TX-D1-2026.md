# Distributor agreement DA-TX-D1-2026: Lone Star Retail Partners (TX-D1)

Parties: Acme Beverages (supplier) and Lone Star Retail Partners (distributor ID TX-D1), Modern Trade distributor for Texas.

Term: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

## 1. Scope

TX-D1 serves supermarket chains, hypermarkets and convenience store chains in Texas. It distributes the full Acme Beverages range except Harbor beer, which in Texas goes only through TX-D2 and TX-D3.

Priority SKUs: Volt Original 473ml Can (VT-ORIG-473C), Volt Zero Sugar 473ml Can (VT-ZERO-473C), Fizzo Cola 355ml Can (FZ-COLA-355C), Aqua Pura Still 500ml PET (AP-STILL-500P).

## 2. Trade terms

Off-invoice discount: 8% on all SKUs. Volt range listing fee support: USD 1,200 per chain per year.

Promo co-funding: 50% of the shelf discount for approved Volt promotions, 40% for other brands.

## 3. Volt allocation clause

Volt Original 473ml Can (VT-ORIG-473C) is supplied to Texas from the regional can line, which runs Volt production in fixed slots. During allocation periods, Acme Beverages may cap Volt shipments to each TX distributor at its share of the last 8 weeks of depletions.

Allocation in force: from 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-30, the can line is under a planned maintenance window. VT-ORIG-473C shipments to all three TX distributors (TX-D1, TX-D2, TX-D3) were reduced to about 15% of normal volume for weeks 2026-10-19, 2026-10-26 and 2026-11-02.

Expected result: low stock cover of Volt Original across all three TX distributors (around 2 to 3 days of cover by 2026-11-08). The next full production slot for the 473ml can is the week of 2026-11-16, with 5-day lead time to Texas.

During allocation, planners may raise replenishment requests above the allocation cap only with approval of a supply planning manager, as these requests take volume from other markets.

## 4. Ordering and delivery

Regular orders: twice a week. Minimum order: 50 cases per SKU (POL-REPL-2026).

Lead time: 5 days to the TX-D1 warehouse in Lone Star.

Cover target: 21 days (Modern Trade).

## 5. Credit terms

Payment terms: 45 days. Credit limit: USD 140,000. TX-D1 is in good standing, with no overdue invoices as of 2026-11-01.

## 6. Service levels

Fill rate from Acme Beverages to TX-D1: 97% target. Fill rate from TX-D1 to stores: 95% target. Out-of-stock above 3% of audited stores for any priority SKU triggers a joint recovery plan.

## 7. Contacts

TX market sales lead, TX supply planner, TX-D1 commercial director (roles only).
