# Product spec: Fizzo Cola 600ml PET (FZ-COLA-600P)

## Identification

SKU: FZ-COLA-600P. Brand: Fizzo. Category: Carbonated soft drinks. Product name: Fizzo Cola 600ml PET.

## Fizzo pack range (pack hierarchy)

Fizzo Cola 355ml Can (FZ-COLA-355C): 24 per case, USD 11.50 per case.

Fizzo Cola 600ml PET (FZ-COLA-600P): 12 per case, USD 9.80 per case.

Fizzo Cola 2L PET (FZ-COLA-2000P): 6 per case, USD 10.40 per case.

Fizzo Zero 355ml Can (FZ-ZERO-355C): 24 per case, USD 11.90 per case.

Fizzo Lime 600ml PET (FZ-LIME-600P): 12 per case, USD 9.60 per case.

## Pack details for FZ-COLA-600P

Unit: 600ml single-serve PET bottle, 28mm closure.

Case: 12 bottles, shrink-wrapped tray.

Pallet: 120 cases per pallet (6 layers × 20 cases).

## Product

Cola-flavoured carbonated soft drink, 10.6 g sugar per 100 ml. Nutrition Facts labelling follows FDA rules in every market; some cities apply sugary-drink taxes or menu warnings (for example in parts of CA and NY), so price points must include them.

Shelf life: 6 months. Minimum remaining shelf life at delivery to distributor: 4 months.

## Channel guidance

The 600ml PET is the "on-the-go" pack. It is the top-selling Fizzo pack in Traditional Trade convenience stores and gas-station c-stores. In New York (NY), it is the reference pack for price-point promotions at single-serve price points.

## Substitution

When FZ-COLA-600P is out of stock, shoppers trade down to Fizzo Cola 355ml Can or switch to a competing cola. About half of lost volume goes to competitors.

## Supply

Produced on the Northeast PET line for NY and IL, the West PET line for CA and AZ, and the Southeast PET line for GA, FL and NC. Lead time to NY distributors: 4 days.
