# Acme Beverages distributor replenishment policy 2026 (POL-REPL-2026)

Owner: Head of Supply Planning, Acme Beverages. Applies to all 8 markets (CA, TX, IL, GA, NY, AZ, FL, NC) and all 24 distributors.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for proposing, approving and shipping replenishment to distributors. Its goal is to keep distributors in stock without overloading their working capital or Acme Beverages credit exposure.

## 2. Stock cover definition

Stock cover in days = (on-hand cases + in-transit cases) / average daily depletions of the last 8 weeks. Average daily depletions = average weekly depletions of the last 8 weeks / 7.

Stock cover is computed per distributor and SKU from the weekly inventory snapshot.

## 3. Cover targets by channel

Modern Trade distributors (D1): target cover 21 days.

Traditional Trade distributors (D2): target cover 14 days.

On-Premise distributors (D3): target cover 10 days.

## 4. Out-of-stock risk

A position is AT RISK when stock cover is below 7 days (the OOS risk threshold). A position is BELOW TARGET when cover is at or above 7 days but under the channel target. Otherwise it is OK.

Every AT RISK position must get a replenishment proposal or a documented decision not to replenish within one business day.

## 5. Replenishment quantity

Suggested quantity = (channel target cover days × average daily depletions) − (on-hand + in-transit), rounded up to a full case.

Minimum order: 50 cases per SKU per request. Requests below 50 cases are rounded up to 50 when the position is AT RISK, and otherwise deferred to the next regular order.

Active promotions: during an active promotion the planner may add the measured uplift to date (not the forecast uplift) to the average daily depletions.

## 6. Approval thresholds

Up to 500 cases per request: the market supply planner approves.

Above 500 cases per request: a supply planning manager must approve.

Any request for a distributor on credit hold: see section 8. No planner can approve it.

## 7. Lead times (order to delivery at distributor warehouse)

CA: 3 days. TX: 5 days. IL: 5 days. GA: 6 days. NY: 4 days. AZ: 5 days. FL: 6 days. NC: 7 days.

Positions with stock cover below the market lead time are expected to stock out before the replenishment lands. Flag these as URGENT and consider a transfer between distributors in the same market.

## 8. Credit hold rule

No top-up shipments to a distributor on credit hold. A distributor is placed on credit hold by Acme Beverages Finance when its overdue balance is older than 45 days, or its exposure is above the credit limit in its distributor agreement.

While a distributor is on credit hold, replenishment requests must be rejected with reason "distributor on credit hold". The only exception is a cash-in-advance order approved in writing by the market finance controller.

Planners should cover the shopper demand through the other distributors of the market where possible, within their own cover targets.

The credit hold status and its start date are recorded in the distributor agreement file and in the Finance credit report.

## 9. Transfers between distributors

Stock can be transferred between distributors in the same market when one is above 150% of its cover target and another is AT RISK. Transfers need both distributors' agreement and the market sales lead's approval. Freight is charged to the receiving distributor.

## 10. Decision record

Every approval, rejection or deferral must state a reason. Rejections because of credit hold, allocation limits or pending promo reviews must reference the governing document (agreement ID, promo brief ID or this policy).

## 11. Review

This policy is reviewed every year in December. Market-specific rules are in the market playbooks.
