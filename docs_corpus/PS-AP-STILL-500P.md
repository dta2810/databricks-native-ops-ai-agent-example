# Product spec: Aqua Pura Still 500ml PET (AP-STILL-500P)

## Identification

SKU: AP-STILL-500P. Brand: Aqua Pura. Category: Water. Product name: Aqua Pura Still 500ml PET.

Aqua Pura Still 1.5L PET (AP-STILL-1500P): 12 per case, USD 6.90 per case.

Aqua Pura Sparkling 500ml Glass (AP-SPARK-500G): 12 per case, USD 12.80 per case.

## Pack details for AP-STILL-500P

Unit: 500ml lightweight PET bottle (30% recycled PET), sports cap optional by market.

Case: 24 bottles, shrink-wrapped tray.

Pallet: 84 cases per pallet (7 layers × 12 cases).

## Product

Purified still drinking water, filtered and ozonated. Zero calories, no added minerals.

Shelf life: 18 months. Minimum remaining shelf life at delivery to distributor: 12 months.

Storage: away from direct sunlight and from chemicals or strong odours.

## Channel guidance

Modern Trade is the strongest channel for Aqua Pura (about 1.3x the volume per outlet of other brands), mostly in multipacks and the 1.5L family pack. The 500ml is the main pack for convenience stores, gyms, schools and offices.

California (CA) is the largest Aqua Pura market. In California, demand for the 500ml rises with temperature and with school and office calendars.

## Substitution

Water shoppers switch brands easily. When AP-STILL-500P is out of stock, most of the volume is lost to other water brands. Keep AP-STILL-500P at or above target cover in California, especially in Traditional Trade.

## Supply

Produced on the West PET line in California. Lead time to California distributors: 3 days.
