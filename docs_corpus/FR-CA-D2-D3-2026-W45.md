# Distributor field report: CA-D2 and CA-D3

Report date: 2026-11-06. Visit window: 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-05. Prepared by: California field sales team, Traditional Trade and On-Premise.

## Headline

PR-0422 (Sunny Grove Orange 1L Tetra, SG-ORNG-1000T, Display + feature at 20% off) is well executed at Golden Coast Convenience Network (CA-D2) and Golden Coast HoReCa Supply (CA-D3).

Measured uplift to date: about +24% at CA-D2 and +18% at CA-D3 versus the 8-week pre-promo baseline. Both are below the +45% market forecast, but both are on track for the channel. Traditional Trade and On-Premise were never expected to carry the full display effect.

## CA-D2 Golden Coast Convenience Network (Traditional Trade)

Counter display SG-DSP-CTR-06 found in 74% of the 180 visited convenience stores, above the 70% requirement. Price wobblers and the updated price board were present in most of them.

The free-case incentive (one free case per 10 cases bought) is popular with store owners. Average order size per store is up from 3 to 5 cases.

Risk: out-of-stock. Several store owners reported that the CA-D2 truck arrived with fewer SG-ORNG-1000T cases than ordered in the week of 2026-11-02. The CA-D2 warehouse is close to the 7-day OOS risk threshold for this SKU. CA-D2 asked for an urgent top-up of SG-ORNG-1000T.

Also note: Aqua Pura Still 500ml PET (AP-STILL-500P) was missing in about one in five visited convenience stores. CA-D2 confirmed low warehouse stock on that SKU too.

## CA-D3 Golden Coast HoReCa Supply (On-Premise)

1L Tetra listed on breakfast menus in 64 partner hotels and cafés, above the 60 requirement. Table tents SG-TT-HRC in place in most visited outlets.

Uplift is lower than in convenience stores, as expected for On-Premise. Hotel buyers order weekly and do not hold stock.

Risk: the CA-D3 warehouse had zero on-hand SG-ORNG-1000T at the end of the week, with only one inbound delivery in transit. Two hotel groups said they would delist the item from the breakfast buffet if supply is not stable.

## Recommendations

Rebalance SG-ORNG-1000T stock from CA-D1, which is overstocked because its display execution failed (see FR-CA-D1-2026-W45), toward CA-D2 and CA-D3.

Raise a replenishment request for CA-D2 and CA-D3 under the replenishment policy POL-REPL-2026. Both distributors are in good credit standing and have no hold on their accounts.

Keep the promo running at both distributors. Do not change the discount depth.
