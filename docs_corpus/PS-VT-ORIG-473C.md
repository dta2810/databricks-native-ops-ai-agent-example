# Product spec: Volt Original 473ml Can (VT-ORIG-473C)

## Identification

SKU: VT-ORIG-473C. Brand: Volt. Category: Energy drinks. Product name: Volt Original 473ml Can.

Sister SKUs: Volt Zero Sugar 473ml Can (VT-ZERO-473C), Volt Tropical 250ml Can (VT-TROP-250C).

## Pack hierarchy

Unit: 473ml aluminium can, sleek format, resealable tab not included.

Inner pack: 4-pack cardboard wrap (consumer multipack, optional by market).

Case: 24 cans per case (6 × 4-pack or loose tray with film).

Pallet: 90 cases per pallet (9 layers × 10 cases).

List price per case: USD 26.40.

## Product

Carbonated energy drink with caffeine (32 mg per 100 ml, 151 mg per can), taurine and B vitamins. Not recommended for children, pregnant women or people sensitive to caffeine. Mandatory high-caffeine warning on pack in all markets.

Shelf life: 12 months from production. Minimum remaining shelf life at delivery to distributor: 8 months.

Storage: dry, below 30 °C, away from direct sunlight.

## Supply

Produced on the regional 473ml can line, shared with Harbor Lager 473ml Can (HB-LAGR-473C). The line runs Volt in fixed production slots, so short-notice increases are limited. Planned maintenance windows lead to allocation periods (see the Volt allocation clause in DA-TX-D1-2026).

## Channel guidance

Traditional Trade is the strongest channel for Volt (about 1.2x the volume of other brands per outlet), driven by single-can impulse purchases in convenience stores.

Modern Trade: chilled placement at checkout coolers. On-Premise: limited, mainly convenience and nightlife.

## Out-of-stock impact

Volt shoppers switch to competing energy drinks quickly when Volt is missing. Field data shows about 60% of lost sales go to competitors, not to other Volt SKUs. Out-of-stock on VT-ORIG-473C should be treated as high priority.
