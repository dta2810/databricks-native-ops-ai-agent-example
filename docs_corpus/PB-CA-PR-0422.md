# Promo brief PR-0422: Sunny Grove Orange 1L Tetra (SG-ORNG-1000T), California

## Summary

PR-0422 is a six-week Display + feature promotion for Sunny Grove Orange 1L Tetra (SKU SG-ORNG-1000T) in California. It runs from 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-29 across all three California distributors: Golden Coast Retail Partners (CA-D1, Modern Trade), Golden Coast Convenience Network (CA-D2, Traditional Trade) and Golden Coast HoReCa Supply (CA-D3, On-Premise).

The shopper offer is 20% off the regular shelf price, supported by a secondary floor display and a feature in the retailer circular or the store price board.

The approved uplift forecast is +45% in weekly depletions versus the 8-week pre-promo baseline, measured at market level.

## Objectives

Grow Sunny Grove share of the chilled and ambient juice category in California during the back-to-school and early holiday period.

Recruit new households to the 1L Tetra pack, which carries the best margin per litre in the juice range.

Build in-store visibility ahead of the December holiday season, when juice demand rises with family gatherings.

## Mechanic and pricing

Mechanic: Display + feature. A 20% temporary price reduction is funded 60% by Acme Beverages and 40% by the distributor through the trade terms in each distributor agreement.

The Display + feature mechanic carries a 1.3x uplift multiplier in the Acme Beverages promo model versus a plain price discount. That multiplier only holds when the secondary display is physically installed. Without the display, the promo behaves like a plain 20% price discount on the regular shelf, and the uplift drops sharply, especially when the feature is also missing.

List price per case (12 units) stays at USD 14.40. The discount is applied at shelf, not on the invoice to the distributor.

## Execution requirements per distributor

All distributors must meet these requirements. Execution compliance is measured by the field sales team in a weekly store visit sample.

### CA-D1 Golden Coast Retail Partners (Modern Trade)

Install the Sunny Grove secondary floor display (POS kit SG-DSP-FLR-24, 24-case capacity) in at least 85% of the 140 supermarket doors on the promo list.

Display installation deadline: 2026-10-23, the Friday of promo week 1. Doors without a display by the deadline must be reported in the weekly compliance file.

Feature the offer in the retailer weekly flyer for promo weeks 1, 3 and 5.

Hold a minimum of 21 days of stock cover in the distributor warehouse during the promo.

### CA-D2 Golden Coast Convenience Network (Traditional Trade)

Place the counter display (POS kit SG-DSP-CTR-06) and the price wobbler in at least 70% of the 2,300 covered convenience stores.

Display installation deadline: 2026-10-26. Store owners receive one free case per 10 cases bought during the promo window.

Update the store price board with the promo price.

### CA-D3 Golden Coast HoReCa Supply (On-Premise)

Include the 1L Tetra in breakfast menus and juice bars of at least 60 partner hotels and cafés.

Place menu table tents (POS kit SG-TT-HRC) by 2026-10-26.

## Forecast and measurement

Forecast uplift: +45% versus the 8-week pre-promo average of weekly depletions, at market level.

Measurement: the uplift by distributor is reported weekly from the governed view of measured promo uplift per distributor. A distributor more than 20 points below forecast after two full weeks triggers an in-flight review with the market sales lead.

Supply planning: the forecast lift was loaded into the replenishment plan for all three distributors. If one distributor under-delivers and another over-delivers, the plan must be rebalanced. Over-delivery in Traditional Trade can push stock cover below the 7-day OOS risk threshold.

## Risks

Display installation slips at Modern Trade accounts, which make up most of the planned volume uplift.

Out-of-stock at distributors where the promo over-performs, as the forecast lift was spread evenly.

Competitor juice promotions in the same weeks at Modern Trade accounts.
