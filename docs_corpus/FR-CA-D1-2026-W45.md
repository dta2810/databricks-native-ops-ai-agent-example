# Distributor field report: Golden Coast Retail Partners (CA-D1)

Report date: 2026-11-06. Visit window: 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-05. Prepared by: California field sales team, Modern Trade.

## Headline

PR-0422 is not executed at Golden Coast Retail Partners. The Sunny Grove secondary floor display (POS kit SG-DSP-FLR-24) is installed in only 31 of the 140 supermarket doors on the promo list (22%), against a requirement of 85% by 2026-10-23.

This explains why Sunny Grove Orange 1L Tetra (SG-ORNG-1000T) depletions at CA-D1 are down about 5% versus the pre-promo baseline, while the promo forecast is +45%.

## What we saw in store

We visited 42 doors out of the 140. Display found in 9 of the 42 visited doors. In 33 doors the display kits were still boxed in the back room or not delivered at all.

The 20% shelf price reduction is live in all visited doors, but without the display the product stays on the regular juice shelf, often in the lower shelf, next to a competing private-label orange juice running its own discount.

The flyer feature ran in week 1 only. The retailer moved the week 3 flyer slot to a competing beverage supplier. Week 5 is not confirmed.

Several stores showed gaps on the regular shelf. Store managers said the 1L Tetra was not reordered because the store team expected the display stock to arrive separately.

## Root cause (per distributor account manager)

POS kits SG-DSP-FLR-24 reached the CA-D1 central warehouse on 2026-10-21, but CA-D1 did not schedule merchandising crews for installation because its merchandising contractor changed on 2026-10-15.

The distributor did not report the missing displays in the weekly compliance file for weeks 1 and 2, as required by the promo brief PB-CA-PR-0422 and by clause 6.2 of the trade agreement DA-CA-D1-2026.

## Impact

PR-0422 uplift at CA-D1 is around −5%, against a forecast of +45%. CA-D1 is the largest Modern Trade account for the 1L Tetra pack, so the market-level uplift is pulled down to about +15%.

Because the forecast lift was loaded into the replenishment plan, CA-D1 now holds excess stock of SG-ORNG-1000T (well above the 21-day Modern Trade cover target), while other distributors where the promo works are short.

## Agreed actions

Install displays in the remaining 109 doors by 2026-11-13, with two extra merchandising crews funded by CA-D1 under clause 6.3 of DA-CA-D1-2026.

Secure a replacement flyer slot for week 5 (week of 2026-11-16).

Hold further SG-ORNG-1000T shipments to CA-D1 until stock cover returns under 28 days, and move available stock toward CA-D2 and CA-D3 where cover is below target.

Field sales to re-audit 40 doors on 2026-11-14 and report display compliance.

## Notes for the revenue manager

The current gap is an execution issue, not a pricing or demand issue. Where the display is installed (9 visited doors), the store-level sell-out is in line with the +45% forecast.

Recommendation: do not extend the discount depth. Fix the display first, then reassess the remaining weeks of PR-0422.
